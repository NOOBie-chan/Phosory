What does Phosory mean?

+ The name "Phosory" is derived from:
  • "Phos" (φῶς) - Greek for light
  • "Ori" (אורי) - Hebrew for my light
Combined, Phosory = “light + my light” → “light that belongs to me” / “light that is from me”.
It symbolizes knowledge, growth, clarity, and digital transformation.

What does Phosory mean for the brand?

+ This isn't about light in a generic sense, it's more personal and purpose-driven
  • Every developer has their own light (skill, talent, potential) - Phosory brings that light out and connects it to real opportunities
  • Clients receive solutions powered by real human potential
It's not just tech, it's people shining through tech

Is Phosory a freelance platform?

+ No. Phosory is not a freelance platform and that's the point
Freelance platforms connect you and leave you to figure things out. Phosory takes control of the process, ensuring projects are properly structured, executed and completed with oversight.

How are developers selected?

+ Developers aren't just picked they're evaluated and refined.
Phosory looks at skill and problem-solving ability. Developers who join are expected to meet a certain standard before working on real projects.

How does Phosory ensure quality?

+ Through proper structure and supervision.
Projects aren't left in the hands of a random developer. There's guidance, review and a system that ensures:
  • Clear requirements
  • Proper execution
  • Consistent standards
No guesswork. No abandoned projects.

How long do projects take?

+ It depends on the scope.
Simple projects can take days to a couple of weeks, while more complex systems take longer. What matters is that timelines are defined upfront and managed, not left vague.

How is pricing handled?

+ Pricing is based on project scope, complexity and requirements.
Instead of random bids. Phosory structres the project first then gives a clear justified cost.

Can beginners join as developers?

+ Beginners can join to learn and grow but they don't just jump into critical projects. They're guided, trained and gradually introduced to real work as they improve.
